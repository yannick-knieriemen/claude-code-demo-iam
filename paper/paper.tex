\section{Introduction}
We solve a stripped-down DICE-style model in 5-year periods from 2025 to 2300
and report results up to 2200. A social planner chooses the abatement rate to
maximise discounted utility; the savings rate is fixed. We compare business as
usual (no abatement) with the optimal policy.

\section{Model}
Gross output is Cobb-Douglas in capital and labour,
$Y_t = A_t K_t^{\alpha} L_t^{1-\alpha}$. Emissions are
$E_t = \sigma_t (1-\mu_t) Y_t$, where $\mu_t$ is the abatement rate. Carbon
accumulates in a single box and warming is
$T_t = \lambda \log_2(M_t / M_{\text{pre}})$. Damages are
$\Omega(T_t) = \psi T_t^2$ and abatement costs $\theta_{1,t}\mu_t^{\theta_2}$, both
as shares of gross output.
